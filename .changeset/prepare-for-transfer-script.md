New bundled Daz script: **Prepare_For_Transfer** (runtime v105) — zeroes the
G8/G8.1 morph dials that also exist on G9 (Areolae, Nipples, Navel, the Breasts
dials, Voluptuous, the length morphs) so a character can be transferred to G9
without them applying twice. Installs into `Scripts/DTH-Character-Studio` like
the other visible scripts; dials a controller still drives are named in the
summary instead of silently surviving. The morph list is editable under
**Settings → Daz scripts** — saving re-bakes the installed script.
